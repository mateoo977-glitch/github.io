Privacy Policy

Mati Backup is a personal-use application used by its owner to back up files to their own Google Drive account.

The application accesses Google Drive only for the purpose of managing the owner's own backup files.

Google user data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

The application does not operate an external server that collects or stores Google user data.

Access to Google Drive can be revoked at any time through the user's Google Account security settings.
